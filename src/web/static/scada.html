[1] PROJECT OVERVIEW:

Simulates the Carbothermic Reduction of Silica (SiO₂). We mix Sand (SiO₂) and Carbon (C) in a submerged virtual electric arc furnace to produce Liquid Silicon (Si).

SiO₂ + 2C → Si(liquid) + 2CO(gas) ↑ [DESIRED]
SiO₂ + C → SiO(gas) ↑ + CO(gas) ↑ [VAPOR LOSS]

Challenge: Too cold (<1500°C) = zero reaction. Too hot (>1950°C) = electricity cost explodes & Silicon evaporates into the air as volatile SiO smoke.

[2] THE AI'S ROLE (PPO AGENT):

A trained Reinforcement Learning (PPO) agent operates the furnace in real-time, tuning 3 control variables every second:

  • Temperature: 1000°C – 2200°C
  • Pressure: 0.1 – 10.0 atm
  • Argon Sweep Gas Flow: 0.0 – 5.0 L/m

AI Goal: Maximize Liquid Silicon Yield while minimizing Electricity Cost (₹) and volatile SiO Gas Loss.
